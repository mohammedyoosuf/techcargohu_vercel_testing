Pharmaceutical

At Tech Cargo Hub, we understand the critical importance of maintaining the integrity and safety of pharmaceutical products. Our tailored warehousing solutions for the pharmaceutical industry ensure:

Sanitation and Safety.
We prioritize proper sanitation and safety protocols to uphold the highest standards of hygiene and cleanliness in our facilities.
Temperature Control.
Our warehouses are equipped with temperature-controlled environments above +6°C, meeting industry standards to safeguard the stability and efficacy of pharmaceutical products.
Avoidance of Shortages.
Through efficient inventory management and logistics processes, we ensure the avoidance of product shortages, facilitating timely delivery of medicines to those in need.
Regulatory Compliance.
We adhere to stringent regulatory standards, including Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA), Authorized Economic Operator (AEO), CT PACK compliance, and ISO standards, ensuring full compliance with national market requirements.
Intelligent Warehouse Technology.
Leveraging intelligent warehouse technology, including temperature monitoring systems and automated inventory management, we facilitate seamless execution of orders, even for individual pharmaceutical products.
